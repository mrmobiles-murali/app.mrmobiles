# Mr Mobiles repair-skills roadmap

## Starting point and target

Starting skill level: display, battery, speaker/ringer replacement; phone dismantling and reassembly. The next target is independent Android and iPhone diagnosis, board-level repair and microsoldering through hands-on training in Bengaluru. Budget target: below ₹25,000. Practical bench time matters more than a certificate.

## 16-week practical sequence

| Weeks | Focus | Bench outcomes |
|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | Electrical fundamentals | Voltage, current, resistance, continuity, shorts, series/parallel circuits, safe ESD and battery handling |
| 3–4 | Measurement tools | Correct multimeter modes, diode mode, continuity mapping, DC power-supply current limits, safe boot-current observation |
| 5–6 | Phone power architecture | Battery connector, USB input, charging path, power button, common rails; read boardview/schematic symbols |
| 7–8 | Fault isolation | No power, dead boot, fast drain, no charge, short-to-ground; compare known-good readings and document diagnosis |
| 9–10 | Component-level repair | Connectors, filters, coils, capacitors, fuses; hot-air temperature/airflow control and pad protection |
| 11–12 | Microsoldering | Microscope posture, tip selection, flux, solder wick, jumper wire, connector replacement and pad repair on scrap boards |
| 13–14 | Android/iPhone casework | Real donor-board exercises, charging/audio/display faults, water-damage inspection and safe triage |
| 15–16 | Supervised repairs | Complete at least 5 documented diagnosis-to-repair cases with before/after measurements and quality checks |

## Before paying an institute

Ask for a live bench demonstration and verify:
- Each student has an individual multimeter, DC power supply, microscope and soldering station.
- Students work on real scrap/donor Android and iPhone boards, not only watch demonstrations.
- The course includes schematic/boardview reading, short finding, current-draw diagnosis, charging faults and microsoldering.
- Consumables, donor boards, tool access, batch size, total duration and all fees are disclosed.
- You can attend a trial session and speak to a recent student.

Do not spend the entire budget on a certificate or tool kit. Prioritize supervised bench access and real fault-finding practice.

## Daily practice log

For every case record: device/model, complaint, visual inspection, battery voltage, DC supply voltage/current behavior, diode/continuity readings, suspected section, repair performed, post-repair current/charging/audio/display checks, and what remains uncertain.

## Safety rules

Never inject voltage into an unknown rail. Start with current limiting and board-specific references. Disconnect the battery before resistance/continuity checks. Do not heat swollen or damaged lithium batteries. Practice microsoldering on scrap boards before customer devices.
